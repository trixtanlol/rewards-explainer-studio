Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Rewards Explainer Studio is a personal tool with a single user: its owner. It has no website accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Everything runs on the owner's computer. This information website is hosted on GitHub Pages with no tracking added by the owner; GitHub's own privacy statement covers visits to it.

What data the tool handles

  • TikTok sign-in tokens (access and refresh tokens, open ID, granted permissions) for the owner's own TikTok account, received through TikTok Login Kit. They are stored only in the owner's Windows Credential Manager on their computer.
  • Basic profile info (display name) from TikTok, shown only on the owner's local approval screen.
  • The owner's own videos and captions, which are uploaded to the owner's TikTok account only after the owner approves them.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It never stores the owner's TikTok password.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the tool, which revokes them with TikTok and deletes them locally. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app's settings under security and permissions for third-party apps.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/trixtanlol/rewards-explainer-studio/issues.
