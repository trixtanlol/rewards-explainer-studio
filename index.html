Rewards Explainer Studio

A personal, non-commercial tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

Rewards Explainer Studio is a small program that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner make original, fact-checked explainer videos about the TikTok Pro app and send them to the owner's own TikTok account.

Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before anything is sent. Videos arrive in TikTok as drafts or private posts, and the owner decides whether to publish them.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit: the owner signs in on TikTok's own page to connect their account.
  • Content Posting API: approved videos are uploaded to the owner's account (draft upload, or a post with settings the owner chooses).

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
